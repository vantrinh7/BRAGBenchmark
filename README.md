<img width="468" height="84" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8818ec2e-50c4-4d6e-8d79-80558a8e365a" />This study investigates the cooperative binding of oxygen to hemoglobin, focusing on the application of the Hill and Adair equations to model oxygen-hemoglobin interactions. Protein-ligand binding is a foundational concept in biochemistry with critical implications for human physiology. Deepening our understanding of these oxygen-binding mechanisms supports the advancement and optimization of biomedical applications and therapeutic interventions for respiratory and cardiovascular diseases.
<img width="468" height="135" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e9d2ab18-7de8-4f91-9b71-14a1cebaa405" />
This repository contains the following files:

* Oxygen-hemoglobin binding data: The first sheet contains the raw data points of partial pressure of oxygen pO_2and oxygen saturation Y/1-Y for wild-type hemoglobin (Hb), myoglobin (Mb), recombinant hemoglobin (rHb), and hemoglobin treated with inositol hexaphosphate. The second sheet contains a plot of pO_2  versus Y/1-Y with each data point labeled with its exact x and y coordinates. The third sheet contains plots before and after axes transformation. <img width="468" height="93" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/426e0b73-67b5-47e1-8d25-6bc23b062ad4" />
